Affluenter Vault

A password manager for Android that keeps everything encrypted on your phone. No account, no Affluenter server, and nothing we can see.

See what it does Get help

Coming soon to Google Play · Android 10 and later

Everything in one locked place

Logins, cards, notes, passkeys, 2FA codes and private files, filled in where you need them.

Autofill everywhere

Fills usernames and passwords in apps and browsers, and in Android's sign-in sheet on Android 14 and later.

Passkeys

Create and use passkeys on Android 14 and later, kept in your encrypted vault and confirmed with your fingerprint or password every time.

Create a strong password

On any sign-up or change-password form, one tap makes a strong password, saves it first and then fills it in, so it's never lost.

2FA codes built in

Keep two-factor codes next to your logins and fill them straight into the code box. Bring codes over from your authenticator app or send them to one. A codes screen and widget too.

Quick save

Tap Save in Android's prompt and the login goes straight into your vault, with Undo if you change your mind.

Fingerprint and PIN unlock

Open the vault in a second. The key stays in your phone's secure hardware.

Security

Finds weak, reused and breached passwords, flags sites that offer 2FA, and merges duplicates. Then it walks you through fixing them, step by step, opening each site's change-password page and keeping the new password waiting until it works.

Secure Files

Scans, photos and documents, each encrypted separately with your vault key.

Backups and Drive sync

Encrypted backups wherever you choose, optional daily copies, a safety copy before every restore with Undo, and sync between phones through your own Google Drive (Pro).

Easy to switch

Bring in logins from Google, Samsung Pass, Bitwarden, LastPass, KeePass, Firefox and 1Password, and 2FA codes from your authenticator app.

Travel Mode

Move chosen items and files off the phone before a trip and bring them back later (Pro).

Emergency Kit

A printable kit and an emergency backup so a trusted person can reach your vault if they ever need to.

Private by design

Your vault is encrypted on your phone. Affluenter never receives it, so there is nothing for us to lose, sell or hand over.

AES-256-GCM and Argon2idEvery item and file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your master password with Argon2id, a slow, memory-hard method built to resist guessing.
No account, no serverNothing to sign up for. Your master password never leaves your phone, and we can't reset it.
No ads, no analyticsNo analytics, advertising IDs, location or contacts. Optional online checks send only what the privacy policy lists.
Breach checks done rightOnly the first 5 characters of a password's SHA-1 hash are sent to Have I Been Pwned, never the password.

Free, with an optional Pro unlock

Core security is always free. Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play, with no subscription.
