Everything in one locked place

Logins, cards, notes, passkeys, 2FA codes and private files, filled in where you need them.

Signs you in

Logins, passkeys and 2FA codes, filled in where you sign in.

  • Autofill in apps, browsers and Android's sign-in sheet
  • Passkeys on Android 14 and later
  • 2FA codes fill straight into the code box
  • Quick save from Android's prompt, with Undo

Fixes weak passwords

Security finds what needs work, then walks you through it.

  • A strong password on any sign-up form, saved before it fills
  • Weak, reused and breached passwords found
  • Step-by-step changes on each site's change-password page
  • Duplicate logins merged

Locks away more

Cards, notes and private files, encrypted with your vault key.

  • Fingerprint or PIN unlock in a second
  • Secure Files for scans, photos and documents
  • Travel Mode moves chosen items off the phone (Pro)
  • Emergency Kit for a trusted person

Easy to move

Bring your passwords in, and never lose them.

  • Import from Google, Samsung Pass, Bitwarden, 1Password and more
  • Encrypted backups, daily copies and Undo on restore
  • Sync between phones through your own Google Drive (Pro)

Private by design

Your vault is encrypted on your phone. Affluenter never receives it, so there is nothing for us to lose, sell or hand over.

AES-256-GCM and Argon2idEvery item and file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your master password with Argon2id, a slow, memory-hard method built to resist guessing.
No account, no serverNothing to sign up for. Your master password never leaves your phone, and we can't reset it.
No ads, no analyticsNo analytics, advertising IDs, location or contacts. Optional online checks send only what the privacy policy lists.
Breach checks done rightOnly the first 5 characters of a password's SHA-1 hash are sent to Have I Been Pwned, never the password.
