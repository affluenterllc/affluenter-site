Support

Affluenter Vault for Android

Email info@affluenter.com. Please include your phone model, Android version and the app version (Settings → About). Never send us your master password or vault contents; we will never ask for them.

Common questions

I forgot my master password.

We can't recover or reset it, because we never receive it. Fingerprint or PIN unlock, if set up on your phone, still opens the vault, so make a fresh emergency backup from there. An emergency backup can be restored when you set up the app again; its passphrase (kept in your Emergency Kit) then opens the vault, and you can change it in Settings.

How do I move to a new phone?

In Settings → Backup, sync & import, tap “Save an encrypted backup now” and save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone (such as Google Drive). On the new phone, choose “Restore from an Affluenter Vault backup” when you set up the app. Pro users can also use Drive sync.

How do I turn on autofill?

The app offers “Make Affluenter Vault your password manager?” after you unlock. Tap Yes and confirm on your phone’s screen; on Android 14 and later it then opens Chrome’s setting too (Chrome → Settings → Autofill services → “Autofill using another service”). You can also start from Settings → Autofill → Turn on autofill.

Can I import from another password manager?

Yes. Menu ⋮ → More tools → Import reads export (CSV) files from Google / Chrome Password Manager, Samsung Pass, Bitwarden, LastPass, KeePass, Firefox and 1Password. Delete the export file afterwards, because it isn't encrypted. On Android 14 and later, managers that support Android’s credential transfer can move logins and passkeys straight across, with no file (Settings → Backup, sync & import → Import from another password manager).

Can I bring my 2FA codes?

Yes. Menu ⋮ → More tools → Import 2FA codes reads Google Authenticator’s export QR codes and single setup QR codes. Export 2FA codes sends them back to any authenticator app. Microsoft Authenticator can’t export its codes, so add those again from each site.

I restored the wrong backup. Can I undo it?

Yes. Before every restore or import the app keeps an encrypted safety copy on your phone. Settings → Backup, sync & import → “Undo last restore” puts everything back. To add a backup’s missing items without replacing your vault, use “Merge a backup” instead of Restore.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → About → Delete vault erases everything from your phone; uninstalling the app does the same. Backups and sync files you saved elsewhere stay there until you delete them. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from us.

Refunds and restoring Pro

Pro is bought through Google Play and restores automatically on the same Google account. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies.
