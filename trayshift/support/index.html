TrayShift — Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email info@affluenter.com and tell us your phone model, your Android version and the level you were on.

How to play

How do I win?

Tap a piece on top of a stack to send it to the tray. Three pieces of the same color clear. Empty the board to win. The tray holds five pieces; one more and the level is lost. Retry is always free, with no lives and no timers.

What is a Shift?

Every few taps the whole board slides one step. "Shift in" at the top counts the taps left. At "Shift in 1", colored rings show which piece is about to slide into each square.

What do the badges mean?

  • Frozen (snowflake): can't be tapped until a piece leaves a square next to it. If only frozen pieces are left, they all thaw.
  • Locked (padlock): opens when a trio of the color on its small key dot clears.
  • Linked (chain): two pieces joined by a dashed line. Both must be on top; tapping one moves both and uses two tray slots.

How do hints work?

Wait a few seconds and the game nudges a good next tap. After two failed tries on a board, a hint shows on your next try and a Hint button appears under the tray.

What's the tips panel?

Levels 1 to 15 show a short tip above the board, and level 51 one more for the seventh color. Tap the small i button beside "Pieces left" to hide the tips, and tap it again to bring them back.

Levels and the Daily puzzle

How do levels unlock?

One at a time: clearing a level opens the next. A new world opens when you clear the last level of the one before.

How many levels are there?

300 levels in 30 worlds of 10, with more worlds coming in updates. The tenth level of each world is the hardest, and the next one is a breather. From World 6 a seventh color (teal ✚) joins the board.

How do I replay a level?

Open Levels and tap any level with a check mark. Replaying the tenth level of a world plays its fanfare and confetti again.

How does the Daily puzzle work?

There is one puzzle per day, the same for everyone. You get one finished try a day, win or lose, and a win keeps your streak going. The puzzle is made on your phone from the date, so it needs no internet. Boosters can't be used in the Daily.

What is "Your Week"?

Your own scoreboard on Home: levels cleared this week, records (a clear in fewer taps than your best, without boosters), your daily streak and all-time clears. The week resets every Monday. Nothing leaves your phone.

Boosters, coins and gifts

What are boosters?

  • Blast: tap Blast, then tap any piece. Every piece of that color pops, buried ones too.
  • Magnet: pulls in the missing pieces for every color waiting in the tray and clears those trios.
  • Time Freeze: the next Shift waits 3 extra taps.

They sit on the shelf under the tray. No level needs one.

How do I get boosters?

Free gifts: Blast at level 3, Magnet at level 6 and Time Freeze at level 9, each with one free use. Clear the tenth level of a world to choose one of the three. You can also buy them with coins in the Shop.

What are coins?

Coins are earned by playing: 10 for the first clear of a level (+40 on the last level of a world), 2 for a replay and 15 for winning the Daily puzzle. Spend them on boosters in the Shop (Blast 40, Magnet 50, Time Freeze 30). You can also buy coin packs in the Shop, but no level needs them. Coins have no cash value, and nothing is ever charged for losing a level.

What's the Mailbox and the Daily Gift?

Gifts from the game wait in the Mailbox on Home: a welcome note, your Daily Gift and rewards for Daily streaks. Tap Claim to collect. Streak and welcome messages expire after 30 days; a Daily Gift waits until you claim it. Each day you open TrayShift, a Daily Gift arrives: 10, 10, 15, 15, 20 and 20 coins on days 1 to 6, and a gift box on day 7. Missing a day only pauses the calendar; it never starts over. Daily streaks of 3, 7, 14 and 30 days earn extra rewards.

What's in a gift box?

Pick one of six boxes. The odds are: 60% small coins (20), 25% big coins (75), 12% one booster (Blast, Magnet or Time Freeze) and 3% Premium (two boosters you choose). If your last four picks had no Premium, the next one is Premium. Your prize is set before you pick; the other boxes show other draws from the same odds.

Purchases

What can I buy?

Everything in the game can be played for free. In the Shop you can buy, through Google Play:

  • Remove Ads (one time): no more ads between levels. The optional ads for Undo and Continue stay, because you choose those. For 3 days after your first ad between levels, the Shop offers it at an intro price.
  • Starter Pack (one time): 900 coins plus one of each booster.
  • Coin packs: 500, 1,400 or 3,200 coins.

Each one gives exactly what it says; nothing you buy is random. Prices are the same for every player and are shown by Google Play in your currency before you pay. Purchases need an internet connection.

I bought something and didn't get it

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack come back on any phone signed in to the same Google account. A payment that Google Play shows as pending arrives once it is confirmed. Coins are saved on your phone, so coins you already received don't move to a new phone. Refunds are handled by Google Play. Still stuck? Email info@affluenter.com with your Google Play order number.

Ads

Does TrayShift show ads?

Yes, lightly. After level 10 a short ad can appear before a new level, at most once every three levels, never after a loss, never after the last level of a world and never in the Daily puzzle. Remove Ads in the Shop turns those off for good. From level 11 you can also choose to watch an ad to undo your last tap, or to continue after a loss with one extra tray slot once you have cleared about half the board. Those are always optional. With no internet connection there are no ads, and the buttons are hidden. Ads come from Google AdMob; see the privacy policy.

Where are my ad privacy choices?

If you are in a region where consent is required (such as the EEA or the UK), the game asks when it first starts. You can change your choice any time with the Ad privacy choices button on the Privacy screen in the game.

Sound, music and your progress

What do the Sound and Music switches do?

Sound turns the game's sound effects on or off. Music turns the background music on or off; each world has its own tune. The music stays quiet while another app is playing audio and pauses when you leave the game. Vibration is set in your phone's settings.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. Uninstalling the game erases it. If Android's backup is on for your device, your Google account may keep a copy and restore it when you reinstall or move to a new phone.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. You can play every level and the Daily puzzle offline. Ads, the optional ad buttons and purchases need a connection.
