Questions, bugs or ideas? Email info@affluenter.com and tell us your phone model, your Android version and the level you were on.

How to play

What do I do?

Tap a piece on top of a stack to send it to the tray. Three pieces of the same colour clear. Remove every piece from the board to win.

How big is the tray?

Five slots. The tray turns red at four. If a sixth piece would not fit, the round is lost. You can retry as often as you like, with no lives and no timers.

Why does the board move?

On most levels the board slides every few taps. The top bar shows "Shift in N". When it reaches 1, dashed outlines show which stack slides into each square on the next tap.

What do the badges mean?

  • Frozen (snowflake): a frozen piece can't be tapped until a piece leaves a neighbouring square. If only frozen pieces are left, they all thaw.
  • Locked (padlock): the lock opens when a trio of its key colour clears. The key colour and shape are shown on the lock.
  • Linked (chain): two linked pieces move together and use two tray slots. Both must be on top.

Why did I lose with room left in the tray?

The end card explains the reason, for example that a locked piece needs a trio that can no longer be made. Tap Retry to start the level again.

I'm stuck. Is there a hint?

After three failed tries on a level, the game highlights a winning tap for you. Each level also starts with a short tip.

Levels, worlds and the daily puzzle

How do levels unlock?

One at a time: clearing a level opens the next. A new world opens when you clear the last level of the one before.

Can I replay a level?

Yes. Open Levels and tap any level you have cleared. Replaying a world finale (level 10, 20, and so on) plays the celebration again.

How does the daily puzzle work?

There is one puzzle per day, the same for everyone. Your first attempt of the day counts toward your streak, and later tries are practice. The puzzle is made on your phone from the date, so it needs no internet.

Sound, vibration and your progress

How do I turn the sound off?

Use the Sound switch on the Home screen. The game plays on your phone's media volume.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. Uninstalling the game erases it. If Android's backup is on for your device, your Google account may keep a copy and restore it when you reinstall or move to a new phone.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The current version works fully offline.
