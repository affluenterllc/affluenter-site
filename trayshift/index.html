TrayShift

A colorful sort-and-clear puzzle game for Android. Tap pieces into the tray, match three of a color, and empty the board before the tray jams. Then watch out: every few taps, the board shifts.

Made by Affluenter® LLC · Plays offline · Free to play, contains ads and optional in-app purchases

How to play

Easy to learn in the first level, with new twists as you go.

  1. Tap a top piece. It jumps into the tray. Only the piece on top of a stack can be tapped.
  2. Match three. Three pieces of the same color clear from the tray. Every color also has its own shape.
  3. Mind the tray. It holds five pieces. One more and the level is lost, so plan your taps.
  4. Watch the Shift. Every few taps the board slides one step. Colored rings show where pieces will land just before it moves.

What's inside

300 levels today, more worlds on the way, a new puzzle every day, and rewards for coming back.

300 levels, 30 worlds

From Space, Desert and Candy to Neon City, Robot Factory and Northern Lights, each world with its own look and its own music. Every tenth level is a world finale with a fanfare, and from World 6 a seventh color joins the board.

Frozen, locked and linked

Frozen pieces thaw when a neighbor leaves, locks open when a trio of their key color clears, and linked pairs move together.

Boosters

Blast pops a whole color, Magnet finishes the trios in your tray, and Time Freeze holds off the next Shift. No level needs one.

Daily puzzle

One new puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak going and share your result.

Coins, gifts and the Mailbox

Earn coins by playing and spend them in the Shop; coin packs are optional. A Daily Gift waits in the Mailbox, and gift box odds are shown in the game.

Always solvable

Every level and Daily puzzle is checked by a solver, so a win is always possible. Retry is always free, with no lives and no timers.

Fair by design

Ads stay light: a short ad can appear between levels after level 10, at most once every three levels, never after a loss and never in the Daily puzzle. Watching an ad to undo a tap or continue after a loss is always your choice. Remove Ads, a Starter Pack and coin packs are optional purchases through Google Play, with the same prices and the same intro offer for every player and nothing random for money. Coins have no cash value, and gift box odds are published in the game.
