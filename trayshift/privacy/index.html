TrayShift — Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026. Updated for TrayShift version 0.6.0. Applies to this version until this page says otherwise.

Summary

The game has no accounts and no Affluenter server, and we do not collect your personal information. Everything the game saves stays on your phone. Two things connect to the internet, both from Google: the ad library from Google AdMob, which shows ads and may collect some information for that purpose (see "Ads and third parties" below), and Google Play's billing service, which handles purchases you choose to make in the Shop (see "Purchases" below). Without an internet connection the game plays normally, shows no ads and offers no purchases.

This policy applies to the TrayShift app for Android, published by Affluenter® LLC, Chicago, Illinois ("we", "us").

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your progress: the highest level you have unlocked, the levels you have cleared, your fewest-tap records, your weekly scoreboard, your daily puzzle streak and your last daily win date.
  • Your boosters: how many of each you own, and which gifts, rewards and tutorials you have already seen.
  • Your coins and Mailbox: your coin balance, the messages in your Mailbox and which ones you have claimed.
  • Your Daily Gift and gift boxes: which day of the calendar is next, the latest date the app has seen (so turning the phone's clock back doesn't repeat gifts), a gift box you have opened and its saved prize, and the count behind the gift box guarantee.
  • Your purchases: whether you own Remove Ads and the Starter Pack, and the purchase codes Google Play gave the app for purchases it has already delivered, so no purchase is delivered twice. These codes hold no name, email or payment details.
  • Your settings: whether the Sound and Music switches are on and whether level tips are shown.

This is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It holds no name, email, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used only for short buzzes and taps in the game, such as clearing a trio, using a booster or losing a round. It reads no data.
  • Internet and network state: used only so ads can load and purchases can be made, and so the app knows to hide ads and purchase buttons when you are offline. The game itself works without them.
  • Google Play billing: lets the app open Google Play's purchase screen and ask Google Play what you have bought. It is used only for the purchases in the Shop.
  • Advertising ID: Android's advertising ID permission is used by Google AdMob for ads (see below). You can reset it or opt out of ad personalization in your phone's settings.
  • Ad services, added by Google's ad library: on newer Android versions the library also declares Android's ad-services permissions (advertising ID, ad measurement and ad topics, part of Android's Privacy Sandbox), which let Google show and measure ads with less cross-app tracking; you control them under "Ads" in your phone's privacy settings. The library also declares "prevent phone from sleeping" and "run foreground service", which it uses to finish loading or showing an ad. The game's own code uses none of these.
  • The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.
  • Audio: the music is made on your phone by the app; nothing is downloaded. The app checks only whether another app is playing audio, so its music stays quiet; it does not record, read or store that audio.
  • "View online" links open a page in your phone's browser. The browser loads the page, not the app.

Android backup

The app allows Android's standard backup. If you have backup turned on in your phone's settings, Android may save your progress and settings to your Google account and restore them on a new phone. That copy is handled by Google under your Google account settings and Google's privacy policy. We never receive it.

Ads and third parties

TrayShift shows ads through Google AdMob. We use two kinds: ads you choose to watch (to undo your last tap or to continue after a loss) and a short ad that may appear before a new level after level 10, at most once every 3 levels, never after a loss and never in the Daily puzzle.

When an ad is requested, Google may collect and use information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with the ad, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Which ads you see, and whether they are personalized, is decided by Google and by your choices. We do not receive your advertising ID, we do not give Google your game progress, and we do not sell personal information. Google's use of data is described in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and on its page "How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other places), the app first shows Google's consent form and requests ads only if you allow it. You can change that choice later with the "Ad privacy choices" button at the bottom of this screen when it is shown. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your phone's settings.

We have no analytics of our own. Apart from Google AdMob and Google Play's billing service, the app has no third-party code that collects data.

Purchases

The Shop offers optional purchases: Remove Ads, a Starter Pack and coin packs. Purchases are made through Google Play's billing system. Google handles the payment: we never see or store your card, bank or other payment details, your name or your email address. The app receives from Google Play only what it needs to deliver the purchase: which product was bought, whether it is paid, and a purchase code. That stays on your phone; we have no server that receives it. Google's handling of purchase data is described in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Like every Google Play developer, we can see order records in our Google Play developer account (for example an order number, the product, the price and the country), which we use only for accounting, refunds and support.

Children

TrayShift is made for a general adult audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that collects or sends data, such as new ad types, online leaderboards or new kinds of purchases, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play data safety form before that version is released. The effective date above will change.
