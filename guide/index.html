How to use Affluenter Vault

The basics, in five minutes. The same tips are in the app: More → Help & tips. Looking for one task? See How do I…?

Where things are

  • The bar at the bottom has four tabs: Vault (your items), 2FA codes, Security and More.
  • More holds Settings, Help & tips, the password generator, Find duplicates, Recently deleted, Secure Files, Groups, Travel Mode, email masking, Import and 2FA import/export.
  • ＋ at the bottom right of the vault adds an item. The two buttons at the top are Sort (A–Z, Recently used, Recently changed) and Lock.
  • Search looks through titles, notes, groups, tags and every field that isn’t secret.
  • The chips under the search box filter the list: All, ★ Favorites, 📁 Files, each group and your #tags.

Press and hold

A long-press (touch and hold for about half a second) opens shortcuts that save a step:

  • An item in the list: copy its username, password or 2FA code, or add it to Favorites, without opening it.
  • A group chip: edit that group: its name, icon and the fields new items start with.
  • A code on the 2FA codes tab: open its item. A plain tap copies the code.
  • ⧉ next to an address: copy the address on one line, for web forms with a single address box.
  • A folder in Secure Files: rename or delete it. On a file, it opens the same menu as the file’s ⋮ button.
  • The Affluenter Vault icon on your home screen: Search vault, Add item, 2FA codes, Favorites and Generate password (some phones show only four).
  • An empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → Affluenter Vault: a Favorites widget and a 2FA codes widget. They unlock the vault first and never show names, passwords or codes on the home screen.

Buttons on an item

  • ⧉ copies a value. Passwords and codes are marked sensitive and cleared from the clipboard after a while. Some keyboards keep their own clipboard history; you can clear it or turn it off in the keyboard’s settings.
  • 👁 shows or hides a password. Card numbers show only their last 4 digits until you tap it.
  • ↗ opens the website or app, ✉ writes an email, 📞 calls (your dialer opens first), 📍 opens Maps. Websites, emails and phone numbers inside notes are tappable too.
  • Edit lets you change any field, add fields, and move them with ↑ ↓. Old passwords are kept under Previous values.

Filling passwords into apps and websites

Turn on autofill once (the app offers to do it, or see Support). Then tap a sign-in box: your matching logins appear above the keyboard or in a list. Tap one, confirm with your fingerprint or PIN, and it’s filled. When you sign in somewhere new, tap Save when Android asks and the login is stored. On a sign-up form, “🔑 Create a strong password” makes one, saves it first and fills it.

2FA codes

Items can hold a 2-step verification (2FA) code: scan the site’s QR code or paste its setup key when editing the item. The 2FA codes tab shows every code with its countdown: tap to copy. Codes also fill the code box on verification screens. More → Import 2FA codes brings codes over from Google Authenticator.

Security

The Security tab walks you through your vault one step at a time: duplicates, weak, reused and old passwords, missing 2FA and more. Nothing changes until you tap a button, and most steps have Undo. “Full security report” shows everything on one page, and breach alerts check your passwords against known leaks without ever sending them.

Backups

Your vault lives only on your phone, so keep a backup: More → Settings → Backup, sync & import → “Save an encrypted backup now”, saved somewhere off the phone such as Google Drive. You can also turn on Daily copies. Before every restore or import the app keeps a safety copy, and “Undo last restore” puts everything back.

Still stuck?

Search More → Help & tips in the app, read the common questions, or email info@affluenter.com. Never send your master password; we will never ask for it.
